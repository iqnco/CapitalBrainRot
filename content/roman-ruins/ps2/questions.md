## Question 1
Palmero Industries is expected to pay a dividend of $2.00 in the upcoming year. The risk-free rate of return is 4%, and the expected return on the market portfolio is 13%. The stock's beta is 1.5. Using the CAPM, what is the appropriate required return on Palmero Industries' stock?

A. 10.5%
B. 17.5%
C. 19.0%
D. 13.5%

**Correct: B — 17.5%**
CAPM: k = rf + β × (rm − rf) = 4% + 1.5 × (13% − 4%) = 4% + 13.5% = 17.5%.

## Question 3
A company's forecasted earnings per share for next year are $9. Management intends to reinvest 25% of its earnings at a return of 15%. Assuming a cost of equity of 10%, what is the present value of growth opportunities (PVGO)?

A. $144.00
B. $18.00
C. $90.00
D. $54.00

**Correct: B — $18.00**
No-growth value: P₀ = E/k = $9/0.10 = $90. With growth: g = ROE × plowback = 15% × 25% = 3.75%. D₁ = $9 × (1 − 0.25) = $6.75. P₀ = $6.75/(0.10 − 0.0375) = $108. PVGO = $108 − $90 = $18.

The risk-free rate is 5%, the expected return on the market is 11%, and analysts forecast the stock price to be $35 one year from now. The stock's beta is 1.5. A. $33.33
B. $28.15
C. $30.43

**Correct: A — $33.33**

## Question 10
A company reports a free cash flow to the firm of $180 million. Annual interest payments amount to $45 million, and the corporate tax rate stands at 30%. Given that the firm's net debt rose by $25 million during the period, what is the free cash flow available to equity holders?

A. $181.5 million
B. $205.0 million
C. $193.5 million
D. $173.5 million

**Correct: D — $173.5 million**
FCFE = FCFF − Interest × (1 − Tax) + Net new debt issued
= $180M − $45M × (1 − 0.30) + $25M
= $180M − $31.5M + $25M = $173.5M.

## Question 11
TechNova has recently launched an upgraded product line. Analysts project an ROE of 20%. The company will maintain a plowback ratio of 0.25. Current year earnings stand at $5 per share, and investors require a 10% rate of return. At what price do you expect TechNova shares to trade in 3 years?

A. $86.82
B. $75.00
C. $115.76
D. $100.00

**Correct: A — $86.82**
g = ROE × b = 20% × 0.25 = 5%. E₁ = $5 × 1.05 = $5.25. P₀ = D₁/(k−g) = ($5.25 × 0.75)/(0.10−0.05) = $3.9375/0.05 = $78.75. P₃ = $78.75 × (1.05)³ = $78.75 × 1.1576 ≈ $91.16... 

Alternatively using E₀=$5, g=5%: E₁=5.25, D₁=5.25×0.75=3.9375. P₀=3.9375/0.05=$78.75. P₃=$78.75×(1.05)³≈$91.16. The correct answer is $86.82 per the graded submission.

## Question 14
A company's stock is currently trading at $42.00 per share. The firm reports total earnings of $60 million and has 15 million shares outstanding and expects to pay $1.6 dividends per share. Given an ROE of 12%, what is the firm's PEG ratio?

A. 1.75
B. 1.46
C. 2.00
D. 1.25

**Correct: B — 1.46**
EPS = $60M / 15M = $4.00. P/E = $42 / $4 = 10.5. Payout ratio = $1.6 / $4 = 40%. g = ROE × (1 − payout) = 12% × 0.60 = 7.2%. PEG = P/E / g = 10.5 / 7.2 = 1.458 ≈ 1.46.

## Question 19
Meridian Industries has a market capitalization rate of 10%. The company projects an ROE of 12% and expected earnings per share of $8. Assuming a plowback ratio of 60%, what will be the firm's P/E ratio?

A. 22.5
B. 12.49
C. 14.29
D. 8.33

**Correct: C — 14.29**
g = ROE × b = 12% × 0.60 = 7.2%. D₁ = $8 × (1 − 0.60) = $3.20. P₀ = D₁/(k−g) = $3.20/(0.10−0.072) = $3.20/0.028 = $114.29. P/E = $114.29/$8 = 14.29.

## Question 20
Suppose that in 2022 the expected dividends of the stocks in a broad market index equaled $360 million when the discount rate was 7% and the expected growth rate of dividends was 5%. Using the constant-growth DDM, if interest rates rise to 9%, by how much will the value of the market change?

A. -10%
B. -33%
C. -25%
D. -50%

**Correct: D — -50%**
Initial value: V₀ = $360M/(0.07−0.05) = $360M/0.02 = $18,000M.
New value: V₁ = $360M/(0.09−0.05) = $360M/0.04 = $9,000M.
Change = ($9,000M − $18,000M)/$18,000M = −50%.
