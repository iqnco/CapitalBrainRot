## Question 1
Which of the following statements is CORRECT?

A. Profit of a holder of an option at expiration equals payoff plus the premium paid
B. A short straddle profits most when volatility surges sharply in either direction
C. A protective put limits downside risk while a covered call caps upside potential
D. The writer of a call has the right (not an obligation) to sell the stock at the strike price

**Correct: C** — A protective put floors losses; a covered call caps upside gains.

## Question 2
Imagine that you are the holder of a call on AAPL with strike (X) at $50, with a cost (premium) of $4. The breakeven stock price at expiration is ________.

A. $50
B. $54
C. $46
D. $52

**Correct: B** — Breakeven for call holder = Strike + Premium = $50 + $4 = $54.

A. Firms undergoing restructuring

## Question 5
You decide to enter into a protective put where you buy 1 share of TwistCo at time 0 for $60 and buy a put with a strike (X) of $55 for $2. The MAXIMUM LOSS (per share) in this strategy is ________.

A. $7
B. $5
C. $2
D. Unlimited

**Correct: A** — Max loss = Stock price − Put strike + Premium paid = $60 − $55 + $2 = $7.

## Question 6
You decide to do a covered call: you buy 1 share of Portx Company at $52 and sell a call on the same stock with a strike price (X) of $55 for $2.50. The MAXIMUM PROFIT per share is ________.

A. Unlimited
B. $5.50
C. $2.50
D. $3.00

**Correct: B** — Max profit = (Strike − Stock price) + Premium received = ($55 − $52) + $2.50 = $5.50.

## Question 7
On March 1, you enter a short position by selling a June S&P 500 E-mini futures contract at 4,250. The contract multiplier is $50 per index point. You decide to close the position on April 1 when the June futures price is 4,235. Your profit is ________.

A. A gain of $1,500
B. A loss of $750
C. A gain of $750
D. A loss of $1,500

**Correct: C** — Short profit = (Sell price − Buy price) × Multiplier = (4,250 − 4,235) × $50 = $750 gain.

A. 8%

## Question 9
The market capitalization rate of Nvidia is 8%. Its expected ROE is 12%. If the firm's plowback ratio is 50%, its P/E ratio will be ________.

A. 25
B. 14
C. 16
D. 20

**Correct: A** — g = ROE × plowback = 12% × 50% = 6%. P/E = (1 − b)/(k − g) = 0.50/(0.08 − 0.06) = 25.

## Question 11
The company's free cash flow to the firm (FCFF) is expected to remain constant at $30 million per year (no growth). Pirelli's WACC is 10%. If the market value of the debt is $50 million, what is the value of the equity using the DCF / free cash flow valuation approach?

A. 250m
B. 350m
C. 150m
D. 300m

**Correct: A** — Firm value = FCFF / WACC = $30M / 0.10 = $300M. Equity = $300M − $50M debt = $250M.

## Question 12
You are an investor who buys a put on MSFT with X of $45 for a $2 premium. At expiration, the stock price is at $40. Your PROFIT per share is ________.

A. $5
B. $-2
C. $0
D. $3

**Correct: D** — Put payoff = max(45 − 40, 0) = $5. Profit = $5 − $2 premium = $3.

## Question 13
A stock just paid a dividend of D0 = $2.20. Dividends will grow at 8% for 3 years, then 4% forever. Required return: 9%. What is the stock's intrinsic value?

A. $50.99
B. $55.20
C. $47.85
D. $52.44

**Correct: A** — D1=$2.376, D2=$2.566, D3=$2.771. P3 = D3×1.04/(0.09−0.04) = $57.64. PV = 2.376/1.09 + 2.566/1.09² + (2.771+57.64)/1.09³ ≈ $50.99.

## Question 16
For a storable commodity, the spot price is $50, and the 9-month futures price is $54. The annual risk-free rate is 5%, and annual storage costs are 3% (as a % of spot, paid effectively at maturity). There is no convenience yield. Is there an arbitrage opportunity, and what would be the profit?

A. Yes, there is an arbitrage opportunity; the profit will be $4.00
B. Yes, there is an arbitrage opportunity; the profit will be $1.03
C. No arbitrage opportunity
D. Yes, there is an arbitrage opportunity; the profit will be $3.25

**Correct: B** — Fair futures = $50 × (1 + 0.05 × 0.75) + $50 × 0.03 × 0.75 = $51.875 + $1.125 = $52.97. Actual $54 > $52.97, so arbitrage profit ≈ $1.03.

## Question 17
The initial margin requirement on a stock purchase is 50%. You fully use the margin allowed to purchase 100 shares of Broadcom at $20. If the price drops to $15, what is the percentage loss on your equity?

A. 50%
B. 60%
C. 30%
D. 25%

**Correct: A** — Own equity = 50% × $2,000 = $1,000. At $15: equity = $1,500 − $1,000 loan = $500. Loss = ($1,000 − $500) / $1,000 = 50%.

## Question 18
A firm has FCFE next year of €5.00, expected to grow at 4% indefinitely. Required return on equity is 12%.

What is the intrinsic equity value?

A. 62.50
B. 86.67
C. 50
D. 72.50

**Correct: A** — V = FCFE / (k − g) = €5.00 / (0.12 − 0.04) = €5.00 / 0.08 = €62.50.

## Question 19
Which of the following is the main idea behind intrinsic equity valuation?

A. Stocks are correctly priced only when P/E ratios are constant
B. Prices are determined only by market supply and demand
C. Risk does not affect a stock's estimated value
D. Share value should be equal to the present value of expected future cash flows

**Correct: D** — Intrinsic valuation discounts all expected future cash flows to the present.

## Question 20
If a stock's intrinsic value is lower than its market price, the stock is:

A. Risk-free
B. Overvalued and should be sold
C. Undervalued and should be bought
D. Fairly valued

**Correct: B** — If intrinsic value < market price, the stock is overvalued — it costs more than it is worth.
