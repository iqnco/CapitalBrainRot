## Question 1
A quote sheet has 3-month (91-day) T-bill quotes with 4.20 bid and 4.15 ask. The T-bill has a $1,000 face value. If an investor buys the T-bill, what would be the Bond Equivalent Yield (BEY) of his investment?

A. 4.20%
B. 4.252%
C. 4.15%
D. 4.304%

**Correct: B — 4.252%**
BEY uses the ask discount rate (investor buys at ask): Price = $1,000 × (1 − 0.0415 × 91/360) = $989.51. BEY = ($10.49 / $989.51) × (365/91) = 4.252%.

## Question 23
An investor is bullish on BlueMountain Light Co., currently trading at $40 per share. He has $10,000 of his own capital and decides to fully use the margin allowed by his broker, who requires an initial margin of 40%. If the stock price rises from $40 to $50, what would be the rate of return on his investment without margin and with margin, respectively?

A. 25% and 62.50%
B. 25% and 50.00%
C. 25% and 41.67%
D. 20% and 50.00%

**Correct: A — 25% and 62.50%**
Without margin: ($50−$40)/$40 = 25%.
With 40% margin on $25,000 investment ($10,000 own + $15,000 borrowed): buy 625 shares. Gain = 625×$10 = $6,250. Return on equity = $6,250/$10,000 = 62.50%.

## Question 24
An investor decides to short sell 300 shares of RocketFuel Technologies Inc., currently trading at $80. The broker requires an initial margin of 50% and a maintenance margin of 30%. No dividends paid, no interest earned on margin account. At what stock price will the investor receive a margin call?

A. $98.46
B. $89.50
C. $95.00
D. $92.31

**Correct: D — $92.31**
Proceeds from short = $80 × 300 = $24,000. Initial margin = 50% × $24,000 = $12,000. Total in account = $36,000.
Margin call when: ($36,000 − 300P) / (300P) = 0.30 → $36,000 − 300P = 90P → 390P = $36,000 → P = $92.31.

## Question 28
What would be the profit or loss per share to an investor who bought an October expiration Apple call option with an exercise price of $130 if Apple closed on the expiration date at $120? The option premium was $3.00.

A. $0
B. $7.00 gain
C. $3.00 gain
D. $3.00 loss

**Correct: D — $3.00 loss**
Stock at $120 < Strike $130 → call expires worthless (payoff = $0). Profit = $0 − $3.00 premium = −$3.00.
